LED ring

12 LEDs. The bright one follows the knob; it flashes the turn colour while scrolling.

Scrolling

Slow turns move one image at a time; fast spins speed up. Double-tap the knob to switch.

Many PACS use Home and End for the first and last image. Double-tap always switches Smart and Exact.
